## Supporting photography guidance

The linked June 2025 official article recommends engaged activity, uncluttered backgrounds, visible faces, lighting from the front, and internationally understandable gestures. For groups, arrange rows by height and keep people close. Prefer moving closer rather than heavy digital zoom; check focus and take several shots. These are practical photo-quality suggestions, not new mandatory brand rules. Source: https://www.toastmasters.org/magazine/magazine-issues/2025/june/how-to-take-great-photos
