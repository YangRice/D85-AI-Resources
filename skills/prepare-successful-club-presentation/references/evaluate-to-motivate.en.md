# Evaluate to Motivate

Official item: **292A**
Pathways enhancement level: **5**
Source: [official resource](https://www.toastmasters.org/resources/evaluate-to-motivate); [supplied LMS URL](https://www.toastmasters.org/resources/evaluate-to-motivate?utm_source=lms&utm_medium=path&utm_campaign=pathways-enhancements); [official package](https://content.toastmasters.org/raw/upload/292-evaluate-to-motivate.zip)
Duration: **10-15 minutes**
Research date: **2026-11-10**
Printed core outline: **292A p. 5; 292A p. 6**

This is an original coaching summary, not the official manual or a substitute for the speaker's project evaluation resource.

## Purpose and coverage

Show how useful evaluation supports the speaker, develops the evaluator and teaches the audience. Preserve five practices: prepare from the project objectives and the speaker's goals, show genuine interest, personalize language, evaluate observable speech behavior rather than the person, and combine honest encouragement with actionable direction.

Use fictional examples or user-owned, non-sensitive material. Do not ingest identifiable evaluations, health observations, private project files or comments about another member without permission and sanitization.

## Source-specific facilitation

Use a fictional 30-second speech moment and three feedback drafts: vague praise, personal judgment and evidence-based coaching. Ask listeners which version identifies an observable choice, its effect and one possible next step. If the user brings their own material, remove names and details that are not needed.

A compact feedback frame is: objective or speaker goal -> observed choice -> listener effect -> sincere strength -> one prioritized experiment. It is a coaching aid, not a replacement official form or a mandatory club script.

## Coaching additions and output

Produce a 10-15-minute outline with one fictional comparison exercise, two examples of respectful language and a short rehearsal. Speaker notes should separate source principles from optional wording. The output must preserve the speaker's agency, avoid a pass score and direct the live evaluator to the correct official project resource.
