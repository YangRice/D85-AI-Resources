This is an original coaching summary. It is not a current catalog, a Base Camp substitute or an approved translation of the official module.

## Purpose and coverage

Help members understand how the education ecosystem supports their goals: learning choices, Base Camp, progression, recognition and the next question to ask. The August 2025 outline predates later changes, including Education Series enhancements, vintage paths and explorations. Recheck all current program facts on the [Education Programs FAQ](https://www.toastmasters.org/EducationProgramFAQ) and current Pathways pages.

Do not ask for Base Camp screenshots, private project content, evaluation files, account data or proof of completion. Explain navigation at a public high level and refer account-specific or approval questions to the member and VPE.

## Source-specific facilitation

Open with a fictional member goal, then let the audience identify which public information they need before suggesting a learning route. Separate stable questions—skills, preferred practice and time—from mutable facts—available paths, prices, Base Camp behavior, enhancements, recognition and DTM procedures.

Create a dated source card listing the FAQ and any current program page actually opened. If browsing is unavailable, state the 2026-11-10 research baseline and give the exact VPE or official-source question that remains.

## Coaching additions and output

Produce a 10-15-minute outline with one goal-to-learning-choice example, a simple progression explanation, a current-source checkpoint and a next-step question. Mark current paths, interface behavior, prices, enhancements, DTM and recognition processes `VERIFY LIVE`. The output may guide the member but must not access Base Camp, approve a level, purchase a path or reproduce nonpublic course content.
