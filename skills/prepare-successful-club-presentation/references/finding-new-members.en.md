## Purpose and coverage

Show that healthy membership is a shared club responsibility, then connect that idea to respectful ways of meeting prospective members and welcoming guests. Preserve four elements:

- why a club needs enough active members to offer varied meetings and leadership opportunities;
- member-led outreach that fits the club and current public channels;
- a guest experience that is personal, helpful and free from pressure; and
- a current check of any membership-building campaign or recognition program named in the presentation.

Do not request a prospect list, guest contact details, attendance records or private follow-up notes. A current public program page can be discussed; an identifiable person should not become an AI example without their permission.

## Source-specific facilitation

Ask the audience for additional outreach ideas, as the outline suggests, but collect categories rather than names. A safe prompt is: “Where might a fictional club meet people who already want to practice communication?” Sort responses into personal invitations, public community presence, educational events and guest experience. Mark every platform, campaign or award as “verify live” until checked on the current [Membership Building](https://www.toastmasters.org/membership/leadership/club-officer-tools/membership-building) page.

For a guest-experience activity, use a fictional timeline: invitation, arrival, meeting, post-meeting conversation and optional return visit. Let listeners identify one welcoming behavior at each point. Do not upload real guest messages or create a follow-up list.

## Coaching additions and output

Produce a 10-15-minute outline with one audience question, one fictional guest journey and one locally realistic action. Add a source-check box naming the program or channel that still needs live verification. Speaker cues should distinguish official outline ideas from optional coaching additions. End with a member action that is personal and respectful, not a numeric promise or an instruction to contact someone without consent.
