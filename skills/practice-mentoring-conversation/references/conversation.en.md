# Conversation guide

Synthetic opening, with AI as mentee: “Could you write my entire speech? I do not know where to start.” Stop here for the user's mentor response. Do not append the ideal response unless they ask for a demonstration. After a response, inspect whether it acknowledges the difficulty, asks a focused question and returns a manageable choice to the member.

Other synthetic situations: a nervous newcomer, a member who rejects feedback, or a mentor giving too many instructions while the user practices being the mentee. Avoid attributing these behaviors to an actual named person. Feedback examples must quote or accurately describe the user's supplied reply. The character's later acceptance is fictional, not proof of the user's real-world effectiveness.
