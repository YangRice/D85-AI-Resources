# Practice guide

Feedback has no required positive quota. If only difficulties are evidenced, suggest an improvement without inventing a strength. Keep recollections labeled as recollections, not notes or direct observations; text is not audio. Follow the user's current language and format requests, and do not fill unavailable fields.

Synthetic example: a member says their opening is unclear. Ask them to state the central idea in one sentence, then try a short opening. After an actual sample, quote a relevant phrase as evidence and ask for their reflection before suggesting a change. A clearer plan alone is not observed improvement; compare a second attempt before making that claim.

For a meeting role, rehearse one realistic moment rather than scripting every interaction. For missing audio, assess only the supplied words and offer a checklist for human observation of pace or vocal variety. Optional progress note: goal / actual attempt / evidence / reflection / next experiment / agreed contact. A note is member-controlled, not an automatic member record.
