# Relationship review guide

Synthetic case: a mentee has not replied. Ask how long it has been and what contact frequency was agreed; silence alone does not show laziness or rejection. Possible unsent check-in: “Is our current contact pattern still workable? We could adjust the scope or pause if that helps.” Adapt only to the facts provided; do not add obligations or disclose personal explanations.

If the member relies on the mentor to decide everything, invite them to choose one small next practice and what help they actually want. If a mismatch persists, discuss a respectful ending or consent-based VPE-assisted rematch. Do not publish complaints. Safety or serious conflict needs suitable human support, not simulated institutional adjudication. Review whether support is reducing dependency, using actual examples rather than a fabricated score.
