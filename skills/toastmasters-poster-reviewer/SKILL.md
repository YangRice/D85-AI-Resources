# Toastmasters Poster Reviewer

Review a supplied Toastmasters poster independently or serve as the maker's separate quality gate. This skill reviews; it does not alter the poster, produce a replacement, submit approval requests or publish. Read [brand rules](references/brand-rules.en.md) and [review contract](references/production-contract.en.md), both included in portable editions. Baseline: `tm-poster-brand-2026-08-v1`, Brand Manual Version 2.0 Rev. 08/2026.

## Establish evidence and context

Inspect the actual candidate and record its SHA-256/revision. Use the supplied context, brief/copy, intended size, manifest, originals and provenance when available. When an approved brief or protected-material evidence is missing, ask the user focused questions first; continue the available visual brand review. Missing documents are not evidence of incorrect content, unauthorized photos or damaged assets, and do not automatically block the poster. A description or maker's assurances do not substitute for inspecting the actual image.

For a maker quality gate, use a different agent/session from the designer. In portable tools start a fresh conversation with these instructions/rules and attach the candidate/brief only after authorization for any external transmission. Never simulate independent approval in the maker conversation. If the image cannot be inspected, return BLOCKED for image-level review with preliminary findings only.

Classify ordinary club/Area/Division/District, corporate, Gavel or annual District conference before applying rules. Check conference eligibility, not just its title. Never apply the conference custom-brand exception to an October/fall event. Distinguish meeting-topic copy/secondary seasonal illustrations from custom organizational identity.

## Inspect five categories

1. **Brand compliance:** assess each applicable B01–B15 rule with page evidence: official logo variant/proportions/provenance; final-size minimum and clear space; palette/allowed gradients; hierarchy/standard font styles; photography and secondary artwork; approved phrase/tagline; Pathways relevance; corporate/Gavel/conference rules. Photo colors need not match palette. Preferred corner placement is not compulsory. Do not invent a Chinese-font approval list or apply certificates/merchandise authorization to every ordinary poster.
2. **Content accuracy:** for meeting/event posters verify the required meeting brief and, when a keynote speaker or sharing master is featured, their name, approved introduction and exact topic. Ask the user to confirm missing facts or supply the relevant brief; record unresolved content as PENDING_USER, not an overall design BLOCKED or a content PASS. Compare all visible regions against approved text, date/time/timezone, names, venue, meeting IDs, links/contact, spelling, translations and completeness. OCR helps but never replaces readable visual inspection. If the brief or bilingual copy conflicts with itself, ask which wording is intended before declaring the disputed fact wrong.
3. **Visual quality:** inspect at full resolution and intended mobile/display size for contrast, clipping, overlap, hierarchy, glyphs, stray model lettering/fake logos, and safe reserved regions. Compare SVG rendering with PNG when source exists. Editable means text/assets/positions; raster background is not fully editable vector art. For standalone review without an editable-source requirement, absent SVG alone is not a defect.
4. **Protected-material integrity:** compare portraits/documentary photos and official graphics to originals and provenance; no unapproved retouching, cropping, recreated people or distortion. Verify manifest/source hashes when supplied; do not infer preservation merely from visual similarity. Keep exact supplied QR source separate from generated artwork.
5. **QR integrity:** decode every QR on the actual final poster and each QR region; match original decoded payload and confirmed destination, retain modules/quiet zone and proportions. A plausible-looking QR cannot pass. Unavailable decoding or an unconfirmed payload leaves QR integrity NOT_RUN or PENDING_USER; ask for the source/expected destination or a scan confirmation. Do not turn that evidence gap into an overall design BLOCKED or pretend QR verification passed. Report a demonstrated QR defect and its correction separately. Report online reachability separately from payload correctness; no unauthorized navigation to private invites. No QR is not applicable.

## Evidence-based verdict

Use the review-contract report. Cite rule ID/manual page for official findings; label practical recommendations separately. Do not invent measurements for flattened images: exact fonts, physical scale, hashes and permissions may be unverified. Font similarity is an observation, not proof; obtain font/source evidence when needed for typography PASS. Missing physical scale prevents print-size verification, not digital-only review. Unknown permissions are not proof of absent permission; report them as unresolved release evidence, separately from visual design.

Only **Brand compliance** can hard-block the overall design verdict. Use BLOCKED only when the actual image cannot be inspected for brand compliance or a necessary applicable brand requirement remains unresolved; name the rule and missing evidence and ask the specific brand question. A missing approved brief, portrait original, permission record, preservation hash, QR decoder or confirmed destination is not by itself a brand violation or a reason to hard-block the design.

PASS requires actual-image inspection, satisfied applicable brand checks and no demonstrated design defect; it does not certify every content/source/QR fact. REVISE indicates a demonstrated correctable defect. Keep non-brand evidence gaps as PENDING_USER, UNVERIFIED or NOT_RUN within their categories, with focused questions for the user first. If answers remain unavailable, keep those statuses and continue the brand review; do not silently promote them to PASS or overall BLOCKED. Report confirmed content, visual, material or QR problems with corrective advice. Tie the design verdict to the checksum and state its brand/design scope, each category, evidence, fixes and pending questions.

Report external authorization independently: not applicable, evidenced, required/pending or unknown. A conference design may visually pass while its required Brand Team approval is pending; it cannot be called release-authorized. Local PASS is never official TI approval. Preserve missing-approval evidence rather than automatically emailing anyone.

Recommend overlay corrections for copy, spacing, official assets and QR issues before artwork regeneration. Any candidate change invalidates the old verdict and requires full-category review on its new checksum. The default is to stop after three unsuccessful maker review cycles with the latest draft/hash and unresolved issues; honor explicit user authorization to refine beyond that default, keeping changed candidates versioned and independently re-reviewed. The maker must recompute the accepted checksum at delivery.

## Executable image measurements

When Python 3.10+, Pillow and OpenCV are available, use the single image-review checker from this skill directory: `python scripts/check_brand.py --template`, then fill actual observations and run `python scripts/check_brand.py --candidate poster.jpg --observations observations.json`. See `scripts/README.md` for the schema, dependencies and reference preparation; `--rules` exports manual identity and measurement data. The model inspects the actual image and relevant crops, supplies logo search regions, all permitted reference variants, design masks and readable copy, and records uncertainty. Never repair uncertain text or invent a font identity, model ID, source provenance or measurement. Bind each observation file to the actual export checksum.

The checker measures logo detection, original geometry, appearance, clear space, effects and digital size, design-color pixels, tagline and marketing phrases. It calls no OCR or model API and needs no renderer logs or font files. Keep model observations, actual pixel measurements and source provenance distinct. Preserve known defects even when other checks abstain; missing inputs/capabilities mean BLOCKED or NOT RUN for the affected work. Without execution, provide the prepared input and manual fallback, never an automated PASS.

A selected-scope PASS is not a complete visual/brand PASS or release authorization. Assess all remaining brand categories visually using the shared rules; exact font identity, print dimensions, rights and approval require their own evidence. Report unclassified mask pixels, uncertain localization and weak matches. Numerical thresholds are engineering screening settings, not official brand exemptions. Each changed export requires new observations and review. No universal model/platform reliability is established by unit tests.
